# EU-PTDicio+ privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

EU-PTDicio+ is an offline European Portuguese ⇄ English dictionary with no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

- **Nothing about you leaves your phone.** Searches, recent words, saved words and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **No tracking.** The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and it doesn't collect or share personal data.
- **Pronunciation audio (optional).** When you tap play on a pronunciation, the app downloads that recording from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Like any web request, Wikimedia sees your IP address and which file was requested; see the [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). Everything else works without internet.
- **"Look up in EU-PTDicio+".** When you select text in another app and choose this option, the selected text is passed to the dictionary on your phone and is not sent anywhere.
- **Children.** The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/vincentlarkin/PT-PTDicioPlus/issues.
